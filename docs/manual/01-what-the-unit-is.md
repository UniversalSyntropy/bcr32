# 1. What the unit is

One box with two separate devices. B1 switches between them.

| Device | Name on unit | Button colour | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sequencer | ZAQ / ZQR / Zaquencer | pink | 4 [tracks](13-glossary.md#track) × 32 [steps](13-glossary.md#step); MIDI, [USB-MIDI](13-glossary.md#usb-midi) and [CV](13-glossary.md#cv)/[gate](13-glossary.md#gate) out |
| Controller | CTRL | amber | 32 [encoders](13-glossary.md#encoder) + 24 buttons sending user-assigned MIDI |
| Settings menus | — | blue | global, sequencer and controller settings |

Key numbers:

- **Sequencer:** 4 tracks, 32 steps, 16 [drum voices](13-glossary.md#drum-voice) per [drum track](13-glossary.md#drum-track), 12 [pattern](13-glossary.md#pattern) [pages](13-glossary.md#page) × 16 [slots](13-glossary.md#slot), [BPM](13-glossary.md#bpm) 30–300.
- **Controller:** 64 [presets](13-glossary.md#preset).
- **Outputs:** 4 CV (0 to +10 V, 1 V/oct) and 4 gate (5 V or 10 V).
- **Notes:** MIDI note range C2–G8 for pitch steps.
